<p><i>Volume Manager</i> lets you control each app's volume independently. It requires <a href="https://shizuku.rikka.app/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Shizuku</a> to access privileged APIs and requires Android 13 or later.</p>

<p>Adjust app volumes from the main interface, press a volume button to open the app-volume popup, or enable the floating button and Quick Settings tile for quick access.</p>

<p>Features include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Independent volume controls for each app</li>
  <li>Volume control for full system streams, including call, ring, alarm, and notification volumes</li>
  <li>Optional volume popup triggered by system volume buttons</li>
  <li>Optional floating button and Quick Settings tile</li>
  <li>Per-app settings and a global switch for the volume-button popup</li>
</ul>
